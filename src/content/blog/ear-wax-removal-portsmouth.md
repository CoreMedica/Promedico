Blocked, muffled or uncomfortable ears are one of the most common reasons people in Portsmouth look for professional ear care. This guide explains what causes earwax build-up, why microsuction is the method we recommend, and what to expect when we visit you at home.

## Why earwax builds up

Earwax is produced naturally by the ear canal and normally clears itself over time, trapping dust and debris along the way. Build-up tends to happen when that natural clearing process is disrupted — commonly through narrow or curved ear canals, regular use of hearing aids or earbuds, or repeated cotton bud use, which usually pushes wax deeper rather than removing it.

Left untreated, build-up can lead to a blocked or full feeling in the ear, reduced hearing, discomfort, or a ringing sensation. These symptoms can have other causes too, which is why an assessment matters before any treatment is attempted.

## Microsuction: the method we use in Portsmouth

We use microsuction for the great majority of patients. It's a dry technique carried out under direct visualisation, using gentle suction to remove wax without water or instruments entering the ear canal blindly. Because it's dry, it avoids the infection risk associated with older water-based methods like syringing, which we don't offer.

Microsuction is generally considered suitable for a wider range of patients than syringing, including those with a history of ear infections or a perforated eardrum — though this is always confirmed at assessment, not assumed in advance.

## What happens during a home visit

Your practitioner will start by asking about your symptoms and relevant history — including any ear pain, discharge, previous ear surgery, or dizziness — before examining your ear canal to check whether wax is present and whether removal is appropriate.

If microsuction is suitable, it's carried out there and then using portable specialist equipment, under direct visualisation throughout. You may notice suction noise or mild movement during the procedure, and you should feel able to ask questions at any point. Afterwards, you'll be given aftercare advice, and if wax isn't found to be the cause of your symptoms, you'll be told what to consider next rather than left without an answer.

## NHS vs. private earwax removal in Portsmouth

Most Portsmouth GP practices have, in line with national NHS guidance, stopped offering routine earwax removal. For patients who previously relied on their GP for this — particularly hearing aid wearers and older adults with recurrent build-up — that's left a real gap.

Private microsuction is recognised by NICE as an appropriate first-line treatment, and doesn't require a GP referral. A Portsmouth home visit gives patients a direct route to assessment and treatment without needing to travel or wait for a hospital referral.
